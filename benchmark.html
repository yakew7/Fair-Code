Benchmark Results Dashboard

The benchmark harness runs five mitigation strategies across three model families on all seven audits, scored on six fairness metrics with a bootstrap confidence interval and a permutation-test p-value. This page explores those same numbers interactively - filter by audit, strategy, model, protected attribute, and metric instead of grepping CSVs.

🔒 Everything below runs locally in your browser - the bundled CSVs are fetched from this same site, and a dropped file never leaves your machine.

⊕

Or drop your own results_fairness.csv / results_performance.csv, or browse

Any export produced by faircode benchmark works - the dashboard detects which file is which from its columns.

⚠ small sample = one compared group has fewer than 30 rows; treat its CI and p-value as unreliable.
